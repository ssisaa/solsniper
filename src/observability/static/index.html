Candles

PAPER rule … … … cfg …
How Candles works Every 0.5s the bot reads pump's 1-second candles for each watched coin (the youngest active Mayhem coins, up to Coins, plus any Pinned mints). Each new finished candle is checked against the rule. own_candle (default): BUY when the candle closed at least Min Δ% above its open, SELL/close when it closed that much below. literal is the original spec (open vs previous close); pump's feed always sets open = previous close, so it only fires on the stream-built fallback. Paper (default): each signal builds and signs the real pump transaction (never sent) to measure speed, then prices the fill on pump's curve 0.45s later, fees and slippage included. Entries are blocked while the feed is degraded or stale, during the 2s cooldown, past the daily loss limit, or with the kill switch on. A coin that pauses or completes while held is closed on paper and flagged; the worst-case figure counts those as total losses. Ladder (inverse rule only): one lot per Reds per lot reds in a row — with 3: buy on red 1, wait on reds 2–3, buy on red 4, wait 5–6, buy on red 7 … — up to Max lots and the Total cap; after that no more buys until the trade is sold or the coin pauses. A green candle resets the red count. The first green (or Greens to sell in a row) sells all lots together as one trade — it never sells on red. With Sell only in profit (on by default) a green sells only when the whole trade is up by Min profit % after fees; otherwise it keeps holding (and keeps laddering) until a later green does. A paused coin, Stop loss % and Max hold s still close it. Pause guard: pump pauses a Mayhem coin once the SOL in its curve drains toward 0, and a paused coin often cannot be sold. So no buy while the curve holds less than Min reserve SOL, and a held coin is sold at once if it drops below Exit reserve SOL. Stop loss % (0 = off) sells everything early. Live lots: lot 1 = Max position, each extra lot the same (fixed) or doubling (martingale). Live: the Live button (with a confirm) sends real trades — BUY spends Max position through the same fast pump route as Mayhem2, SELL sells 100%. This switch works on its own — Auto Trade is not needed (Auto Trade is for the Live Controls source, e.g. Mayhem2). No circuit breaker: losses in a row do not pause Candles (its daily loss limit still applies); one trade at a time with Mayhem2. A live trade is closed by its SELL candle, the coin pausing, Max hold, or your own Sell — no other rule. Config: config/candle.yaml (reloaded within 1s). Report: venv/bin/python scripts/candle_viability.py.

        Mayhem2

        Mayhem_Mode ↗ source … board … max … …
        To trade live: pick a strategy, switch Dry run OFF, then Auto Trade ON.
        How Mayhem2 works Dry run is the default (Settings → Mayhem2): with mayhem2 picked in Live Controls, every signal is built, signed and simulated on the paid RPC but never sent, and trades are paper, valued at what pump's curve would pay. For real trades, untick Dry run and turn Auto Trade on. Trades are built and signed here and sent straight to the paid RPC (about 0.2s), not through PumpPortal (about 1s to build and 3s to land); the "Speed & results" box shows the measured times. Every 0.8s the bot reads pump's Mayhem board and lists the pump coins younger than 3 minutes that are active. For each one it reads Mayhem_Mode's position (unrealized $ and %). It pins the youngest active coin Mayhem_Mode is holding, one coin at a time. On the pinned coin it reads Mayhem_Mode's trades every 0.25s and also watches them on-chain. When Mayhem_Mode BUYS, the bot buys Max position (if it is not already holding). When Mayhem_Mode SELLS, the bot sells 100%. Only trades made after the pin count. Each signal acts once: more BUYs are ignored while the bot is buying, holding or selling, and after the coins are sold the next BUY buys again. Selling for profit: on Mayhem_Mode's SELL the bot sells at once only if the trade is at least +3% (a real profit after the ~2% sell fee); otherwise it waits up to 10s for +3%, then sells anyway. Without any signal it also sells at +20% (take profit), when half of a gain over +10% is gone (trail), at −15% (stop), and after 25s held (half of Mayhem coins pause by ~34s and a paused coin often cannot be sold). These exits run even with Auto Trade off. In parallel it checks the coin's mayhem-state every 0.5s. Paused or completed, or Mayhem_Mode no longer holding the coin, sells what the bot holds, unpins it, and moves to the next new coin. A coin is never pinned twice, even after a restart. One trade per coin: once the bot's trade on the pinned coin closes, at a gain or a loss, it unpins and moves to the next new coin; it never buys that coin again. With Auto Trade and Dry run off, the selected strategy's paper trade decides this. Ladder strategy: Mayhem_Mode's BUY buys; its SELL sells only when the whole trade is at least +3%, otherwise the bot keeps holding; a later BUY buys again only when the price is below the bot's last buy price, up to 3 buys in one trade (each buy is Max position). Ladder has no stop and no 25s max hold: it sells at a profit (SELL signal, +20% take profit, or the trail while still above +3%), or when pump pauses / completes the coin, or Mayhem_Mode stops holding it. Every buy and sell pays fees, so each copied round trip costs about 2% plus network fees.
